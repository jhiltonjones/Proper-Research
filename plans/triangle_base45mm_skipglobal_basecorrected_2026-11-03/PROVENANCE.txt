ACCELERATION SAFETY FACTOR -- DEVIATES FROM THE OTHER 3 PLANS:
  Used:                acceleration_safety_factor = 0.65 (not the CLI
                       default 0.80 used by rectangle, triangle-25mm and
                       triangle-35mm).
  Why:                 at 0.80, Layer 3 (time-parameterization) raised
                       "MPC-sampled trajectory violates a state acceleration
                       limit: maximum ratio=1.206" -- the 45mm triangle's
                       larger, tighter corners demand more joint
                       acceleration than the default 80%-of-hardware-limit
                       budget allows at this path's own time-optimal
                       duration. 0.8/1.206 ~= 0.663 is the breakeven; 0.65
                       was chosen as a value just below that with a small
                       margin, confirmed empirically to succeed (resulting
                       maximum_acceleration_limit_ratio = 0.9527, i.e. still
                       within budget, not borderline).
  Resulting duration:   50.3s (504 samples at dt=0.1s) -- raw (pre-safety-
                       margin) duration was 45.0s; the time-parameterizer's
                       own dilation rounds stretched it to fit the limits.
  Does NOT generalize:  the other 3 plans' own maximum_acceleration_limit_
                       ratio at factor=0.80 is already close to 1 (rectangle
                       0.999, triangle-25mm 0.978, triangle-35mm 0.908).
                       Scaling to factor=0.65 would raise each ratio by
                       0.80/0.65 ~= 1.231x, giving ~1.23, ~1.20, and ~1.12
                       respectively -- ALL THREE would become infeasible.
                       A single shared acceleration-safety-factor across
                       all four plans is NOT possible with this geometry;
                       each plan keeps its own CLI default except this one.
